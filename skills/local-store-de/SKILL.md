# Local store alternatives (Germany)
Use when comparing household goods, cosmetics, sports goods, groceries, electronics, hardware, appliances and comparable consumer products.
1. Confirm the exact product, EAN or SKU, quantity and pack size. Establish the user's city or postal area only if needed for local stock; never assume a specific address.
2. Choose relevant shops, not every possible retail chain: dm, Rossmann, Müller, Decathlon, Edeka, REWE, Kaufland, Aldi, Lidl, MediaMarkt, Saturn, Action, OBI, Bauhaus, Hornbach, and suitable independents.
3. Check public store sites, physical branch availability, online-to-store pickup, current weekly leaflets and genuine promotions. Show evidence and timestamp. If only an indexed snippet exists, say price/stock unverified.
4. Compare **comparable package units** and exact models. For consumables calculate price per kg/L/item and quantities actually needed. Flag minimum basket requirements, app-only discounts, loyalty memberships, coupons and deposit.
5. Calculate online delivered total (product + shipping + mandatory fees) and in-store effective total (shelf price + mandatory fees + meaningful pickup/travel cost if known). Do not make up transport cost or assume zero time cost.
6. Examine returns in calendar days, receipt requirements, return-postage fees for online returns, store-specific voluntary policies and statutory rights. In-store purchases normally do not have the same automatic 14-day withdrawal rights as qualifying distance sales; don't claim they do.
7. Explicitly warn if local purchase offers lower realistic total cost, immediate availability, improved returns convenience or materially better unit value, even if its sticker price is higher. Provide store/source and verified vs tentative status.
8. List checked and inaccessible local sources and report uncertain stock/discounts honestly. Never claim a store has live inventory based only on a search snippet.
